Deep Colony — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Deep Colony ("the game") is developed by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

What the game stores

Your colony progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run servers and do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device information and approximate location. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can deliver the item.

Notifications

Reminders (for example, "the expedition is back") are scheduled locally on your device. They can be turned off in the game settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
